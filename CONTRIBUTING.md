# Contributing

Thanks for helping newcomers!

- **Link to the official source** (gov.ie, citizensinformation.ie, irishimmigration.ie, revenue.ie, hse.ie) for any rule, fee or deadline.
- Avoid hard-coding fees or amounts that change yearly. Link to them instead.
- Keep it practical. Short checklist items beat long paragraphs.
- Tips from personal experience are welcome. Label them as tips, not rules.
- Translations: add a file such as `README.pt.md` and link it at the top of the README.

Found something out of date? Open an issue or a PR. Small fixes are very welcome.
